# The mark

![PortScope icon](../Support/Icon/build/AppIcon.icns)

## Brief

Taken from the `modern-web-design` skill in
[freshtechbro/claudedesignskills](https://github.com/freshtechbro/claudedesignskills),
under its **Bold Minimalism** guidance: geometric shapes and clean lines, a
2-4 colour palette, high contrast, no decorative gradients, and nothing literal
sitting inside the mark.

## The idea

A port is a hole a signal comes out of. A white ring is the port; two pairs of
arcs radiate from it, left and right.

The bilateral symmetry is the decision that took the most iteration. An earlier
version had a single fan opening to the right, which read as an accident and
left the group optically off-centre — the eye kept trying to find the missing
left half. Symmetric emission fixed both, and it scales: at 18 px the arcs
still resolve as a signal, while a one-sided fan collapses into a blob.

The outer pair of arcs is thinner and slightly darker than the inner pair, so
the fan reads as receding without needing a gradient.

## Geometry

On a 1024 grid, centred at (512, 512):

| Element | Radius | Stroke | Span |
|---|---|---|---|
| Port ring | 140 | 76 | full |
| Inner arcs | 250 | 58 | 116°, on 0° and 180° |
| Outer arcs | 350 | 46 | 100°, same axes |

Canvas is `#111216` with a 228 px corner radius — the macOS app-icon silhouette.

| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Ring | `#F2F3F7` |
| Inner arc | `#8FA8FF` |
| Outer arc | `#6B7FEF` |
| Canvas | `#111216` |

Three colours plus a near-black ground, within the skill's 2-4 colour budget.

## Two versions, on purpose

- **`Support/Icon/portscope-mark.svg`** — the full-colour app icon. Ten sizes
  in an `.icns`, generated by `scripts/make-icons.sh`.
- **Menu bar** — monochrome, drawn with AppKit in the same script, not from
  SVG. macOS treats a pure-black image as a *template* and tints it to match
  the menu bar, which only works if the background is genuinely transparent.
  Rasterising SVG through WKWebView composites onto an opaque white page, so
  the first attempt produced a solid white square in the menu bar.

## Regenerating

```bash
scripts/make-icons.sh   # writes Support/Icon/build/ (git-ignored)
scripts/build.sh        # regenerates if missing, then copies into the bundle
```

Both files come from the same geometry, so the mark cannot drift between the
app icon and the menu bar.
